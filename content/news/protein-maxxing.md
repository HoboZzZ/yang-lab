After a day of fieldwork, we harvested some corn from our trap crop for a meal. While husking it, we found an unexpected guest: a **European corn borer**.

A familiar insect for the Corn Entomology Lab, but an unexpected addition to our meal preparations. Well, we’re **Protein Maxxing**.
