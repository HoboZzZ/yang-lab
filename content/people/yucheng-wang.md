My research focuses on understanding and managing key pests affecting corn production, with a primary focus on the biology and ecology of insects. I am particularly interested in how environmental factors like temperature and humidity influence pest behavior, life cycles, and resistance to insecticides. I look forward to learning from the work and experiences of others in the field and sharing my findings. I’m hopeful that my work could contribute valuable insights into sustainable pest management practices!
